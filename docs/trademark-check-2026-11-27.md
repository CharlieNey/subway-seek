## Reading
The literal rule in GO-PUBLIC 1.2 trips: `SUBWAY` is live in classes 9 and 28. But those registrations cover toy figures, toy food, payment cards and a restaurant app, not games. The USPTO registered `SUBWAY BUILDER` for video games in April 2026, alongside the chain's marks. "Subway" here names the New York subway, which the game is about. Risk looks low for a free game that isn't filing its own trademark. A short consult with a trademark lawyer is the formal answer, and worth it before charging money or registering "Subway Seek" as a mark.
