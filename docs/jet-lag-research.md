# Jet Lag game design: research report

**Labels:** **[V]** means verified from a primary source: a cast member's Reddit comment, the official rules or store page, or the fandom wiki's rules tables and episode summaries. **[F]** means fan opinion or a fan's account of something the cast said. **[I]** means my own inference.

- Cast Reddit accounts: u/WheatGerm42 is Ben (his subreddit flair says ":jetlag: Ben"). u/adam_HAI is Adam.
- "r/JLTG/xxx" is short for https://www.reddit.com/r/JetLagTheGame/comments/xxx/
- **Gaps:**
  - The Layover's "Inside the Game Design" episodes are on Nebula only. I found descriptions but no transcripts.
  - TV Tropes blocked the fetch.
  - BoardGameGeek has no reviews of the home game (see §5).

## 1. Their stated design principles

- **The audience has to be able to follow it [V].**
  - Ben: "these games tend to be quite complicated for almost any audience to follow… it's absolutely crucial for us to maintain a coherent throughline." He adds that only "40% of people who start watching… make it to the end" (r/JLTG/1q2e1s6).
  - Ben: "it's important that there's a clear and relatively simple metric for keeping track of who is winning and what players 'want' at any given time" (r/JLTG/1i3yyzm).
  - Adam, on Connect 4: any winning path had to be "visible on a computer screen-sized map" so the game stayed "comprehensible for the audience" (https://www.reddit.com/r/watchnebula/comments/v2kr0z/).
- **The win condition should be real, not points [V].** Ben explained why Hide + Seek scores time rather than questions or points: "the games that translate into the best *shows* are the ones where the win condition is grounded in something real… a ticking clock is intuitive to anyone, even someone only paying 50% attention" (r/JLTG/1ubqwhc).
- **Time pressure is part of the strategy, and length is capped [V].**
  - Tag doesn't guarantee everyone the same number of runs. Ben: otherwise "there's kind of no reason to not just farm coins on your first run."
  - He also said Tag "would start to really feel boring… after 8+ episodes" (r/JLTG/1f78f90).
  - Six episodes is "the sweet spot" (r/JLTG/1e4t7td).
- **Some randomness is welcome; luck that removes choice is not [V].**
  - Ben on luck they like: "sometimes you get a really good start, sometimes you get a really bad start, and that variety can be fun" (r/JLTG/1fjyrqf).
  - Ben on luck they don't: the Switzerland curse dice gave "very very little agency for the hider, and the whole thing is just kind of impossible to balance" (r/JLTG/1qlykk0).
- **Balance is simulated before filming [V].**
  - Kotaku: they play designs through "over and over… balancing, balancing, balancing," and "the rubber-banding is pre-built into the game design itself" ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/youtube-jet-lag-the-game-wendover-board-game-japan-1850641453)).
  - Ben: they test new Hide + Seek mechanics "using google maps in a sort of turn-based fashion" (r/JLTG/1op8fnc).
  - Ben: curses get costed as "X curse costs you X minutes" (r/JLTG/1ixe3sf).
- **Good transit is a hard constraint [V].** Sam: they need "strong frequent reliable, or semi reliable, public transportation." The car-based version "didn't really get as much of strategically exciting game" ([Yahoo/Variety](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/inside-hit-travel-competition-jet-200004834.html)).
- **Simpler seasons tend to be better [F, second-hand, plus V].**
  - A fan reports that at the Season 15 premiere the cast said the simplest games make the best seasons, and Adam contrasted Au$tralia's complexity with "turn up, country is yours" (r/JLTG/1njjzsg).
  - Ben [V]: Capture the Flag was their least successful season, "complicated enough that the only people who continued to watch it were hardcore fans" (r/JLTG/1ilxmho).

## 2. Hide + Seek

**Season-by-season rules [V]**

- **Switzerland (Season 9)** ([wiki](https://jetlag.fandom.com/wiki/Hide_%2B_Seek), plus its Episode 1 page):
  - 2.5 hours to hide.
  - Hiding zone: a ½-mile circle around wherever the hider stood when time ran out. It just had to contain a station.
  - Five question categories, each paying the hider coins: Relative 40, Radar 30, Photos 15, Oddball 10, Precision 10.
  - A 30-minute cooldown within each category (10 minutes for Precision).
  - Coins bought curse dice at 50 each, up to five at once.
  - No time bonuses.
  - Adam said the dice odds were tuned to allow "really devastating curses, but mitigating that possibility" (r/JLTG/1banb8d).
- **Japan (Season 12)** ([wiki](https://jetlag.fandom.com/wiki/Hide_%2B_Seek:_Japan)):
  - 3.5 hours to hide.
  - The zone is now centred on a station. Ben called this "one of the subtler but probably more impactful changes": the hider has "a single specific station that's 'theirs'," which makes station questions "a lot less messy" (r/JLTG/1hp1hy1).
  - Coins and dice were replaced by a hider deck.
  - Oddball and Precision were dropped. The categories became Matching, Measuring, Radar, Thermometer, Tentacles and Photos.
  - Card rewards: Matching and Measuring draw 3, keep 1. Radar and Thermometer draw 2, keep 1. Tentacles draw 4, keep 2. Photos draw 1.
  - Maximum hand size is 6.
- **New York City (Season 13.5)** ([wiki](https://jetlag.fandom.com/wiki/Hide_%2B_Seek_(NYC))):
  - Two teams of two, playing the home game's medium rules with a 1-hour hide.
  - A 5-mile radar asked from Grand Central ruled out Manhattan.
  - The seekers identified the hiders' station from the subway tile patterns in a platform photo.
- **UK (Season 16):**
  - 4-hour hide according to the wiki. One fan summary of the design Layover says 4.5 hours ([tildes](https://tildes.net/~hobbies/1roj/jet_lag_season_16_hide_seek_united_kingdom_trailer)).
  - The same fan summary [F] says:
    - A vetoed question is now gone for good.
    - "Time traps" were added: if seekers pass through a station the hider has trapped, the hider gains time, reportedly growing by 10 minutes an hour. The official expansion rules confirm time traps exist ([rules](https://rules.jetlagthegame.com/expansion)).
  - After everyone has hidden once, the next hider is drawn at random from the two most recent seekers, and the current leader can't be picked (Wikipedia).
  - Stations count only if served at least hourly (Ben, r/JLTG/1itrvdr).

**Question menu: 80 questions in the home game [V, from a fan transcription of the official book]** ([questions](https://jetlag.denull.ru/en/rules/questions/))

- **Matching (20)**, "Is your nearest ___ the same as mine?"
  - Commercial airport.
  - Transit line. Seekers can only ask it while riding. An express that passes through without stopping counts as "no."
  - Length of the station name, and street or path.
  - Administrative divisions, levels 1–4. In NYC, boroughs are level 4.
  - Mountain, landmass, park.
  - Zoo, aquarium, museum, movie theatre, golf course, amusement park.
  - Hospital, library, foreign consulate.
- **Measuring (20)**, "Compared to me, are you closer to or further from ___?"
  - Airport, high-speed rail line, rail station.
  - Borders, sea level, coastline, body of water.
  - The same points of interest as Matching.
- **Radar (10):** ¼, ½, 1, 3, 5, 10, 25, 50 or 100 miles, or a distance of the seekers' choice.
- **Thermometer:** ½ and 3 miles in small games; medium adds 10 miles; large adds 50 miles.
- **Photos:**
  - All sizes: a building visible from the station, the widest street, a tree, the tallest structure in sight, a selfie, the sky.
  - Medium and large add: tallest building, traced nearest street, restaurant interior, grocery aisle, place of worship, train platform.
  - Large adds a ½-mile traced walk.
- **Tentacles** (not in small games), "Of all the ___ within X miles of me, which are you closest to?"
  - Medium: museums, libraries, cinemas or hospitals within 1 mile.
  - Large adds: metro lines, zoos, aquariums or amusement parks within 15 miles.
- **Answering rules:**
  - Answers are due in 5 minutes. Photos get 10 minutes in small and medium games, 20 in large.
  - If the hider answers late, their clock pauses and they draw nothing.
  - A "can't answer" response still earns the hider cards.
  - Asking a question again means paying its cost again.
  - Distances are measured to the map icon.
  - Hider and seekers must use the same maps app, and Street View is banned.

**Why questions reward the hider with cards [V + I]**

- Ben says the deck "is designed to have chaff specifically *as* an obstacle," so the draw-and-discard decisions stay interesting (r/JLTG/1i30icv). The dice version gave the hider little agency (see §1).
- [I] Paying the hider, rather than charging the seekers directly, makes every question a trade. It turns seeker aggression into resources for the hider, and gives a hider who is stuck in place real decisions to make.
- [F] The known weakness: once the 6-card hand is full, extra draws are worth little, so seekers ask as many questions as they like (r/JLTG/1ubqwhc).

**The deck [V]** (counts come from a fan app's reconstruction of the base deck at https://jetlag.denull.ru; the box itself says 125 cards)

- **Time bonuses (55 cards), in minutes for small/medium/large games:**
  - 25 cards at 2/3/5
  - 15 at 4/6/10
  - 10 at 6/9/15
  - 3 at 8/12/20
  - 2 at 12/18/30
  - The Japan season used the large values, 5–30 minutes.
- **Powerups (21 cards):**
  - Veto (4).
  - Randomize (4).
  - Duplicate (2). It can double a time bonus at the end of a round.
  - Move (1). The hider gets 10/20/60 minutes to set up a new zone while the seekers are frozen. Playing it means discarding the whole hand and revealing the old station.
  - Discard 1, draw 2 (4).
  - Discard 2, draw 3 (4).
  - Draw 1 and raise hand size by 1 (2).
- **Curses (24 cards).** Their casting costs come in five kinds:
  - Resources: discard N cards, a powerup, or X minutes of bonuses.
  - Position: e.g. seekers at least X miles away, outside, or heading the wrong way.
  - Gamble: roll a die, and the curse fizzles on an even number.
  - Skill to match: e.g. build a rock tower or film a bird, which the seekers must then equal.
  - Tempo: the seekers' next question is free.
  - Only one curse that blocks questions or transit can be active at a time.
- **Notable curses:**
  - Drained Brain: ban three questions; costs your whole hand.
  - Unguided Tourist: send a Street View image from within 500 ft; seekers must find that spot in person.
  - Right Turn, Gambler's Feet, and Jammed Door (roll 7+ on two dice to enter any doorway).
  - Urban Explorer: no questions while on transit.
  - Egg Partner and Lemon Phylactery: the hider gains time if the egg or lemon is lost.
  - Expansion pack ([rules](https://rules.jetlagthegame.com/expansion), plus the [UK curses](https://jetlag.fandom.com/wiki/Hide_%2B_Seek:_UK/Curses) wiki page): Express Route, Gilded Inquiry, Prosperous Home (zone grows 50%), Tiny Home (zone halves, bonuses worth 50% more), Soothsayer, Queue, and Trickster (the hider may lie).
  - Ben says some combinations that lock the seekers out are "not unintentional," since every player shares the same deck (r/JLTG/1r7g6zq).

**Endgame and scoring [V]** ([rules](https://jetlag.denull.ru/en/rules/))

- Zone radius is ¼ mile in small and medium games and ½ mile in large.
- The endgame starts the moment seekers are inside the zone and off transit, even if they don't know it.
- The hider then stays put at a publicly accessible spot within 10 ft of a mapped path.
- The hider is found when seekers spot them within 5 ft.
- Score is the hider's single best run plus the time-bonus cards still in hand.
- The next round starts from the spot where the last hider was found.

## 3. Mechanics from other formats

- **Tag [V]:**
  - The runner pays for transit in coins per minute and earns coins from challenges drawn from a deck.
  - Vetoing a challenge costs 30 minutes with no transit and no challenges.
  - Curse cards are shuffled into the challenge deck and pay out immediately.
  - Chasers are frozen after a tag. Adam's reason: "so that we aren't just like sitting next to him tagging him over and over" (https://www.reddit.com/r/watchnebula/comments/styorb/).
  - [F] In Tag All Stars, powerups became free but usable once per run, because players had stopped using them when they cost coins (r/JLTG/1nlpaj2).
- **Circumnavigation:** a second challenge done within 300 miles of the first is worth half, and later ones a quarter, to force travel (Wikipedia).
- **Battle 4 America:** each team holds a hand of 7 challenges. Teams can steal a state by "battle" only if they hold two neighbouring states, and a state is locked once a battle is won.
- **Au$tralia:** challenges are wagers with multipliers, and steal challenges put 30–50% of a team's money at stake.
- **Schengen Showdown:** you claim a country just by entering it, lock it with a one-shot secret challenge, and an opponent can steal an unlocked country by completing that challenge.
- **What landed and what didn't [F, reported]:**
  - Simple claim, lock and steal formats are well received.
  - New Zealand had no catch-up mechanic; fans say roadblocks "can only be thrown forward."
  - Snake rewarded sitting still: "the game is called Snake, not Chicken" (r/JLTG/1oqpn6e).
  - Tag All Stars had challenges so easy there was only one tag all season (r/JLTG/1od55kq).

## 4. What made seasons great or flat

- **The cast's own ranking [F, second-hand]:** 1 Hide + Seek Japan, 2 Tag 2, 3 Tag 1 … 7 Hide + Seek Switzerland … 14 Snake (r/JLTG/1njjzsg).
- **Japan [F]:** praised for its mix of skill and luck and for real hider choices. Switzerland's curse dice "felt too randomized" ([review](https://rosymiranto.home.blog/2025/01/31/jet-lag-season-12-review/)).
- **The UK was flatter [F]:**
  - Experienced seekers cut the map in half over and over with the same few questions: small thermometers, radars, the traced walk, the tallest building (r/JLTG/1pwu7zx, r/JLTG/1tl3r6s).
  - Starting in London, the rail hub, gave Adam a first run nobody beat (r/JLTG/1qdw6kj).
  - The endgame feels like an afterthought (r/JLTG/1q7h0va).
- **The cast's response [V]:**
  - Ben: "we already intended for this to be the last time we'd run hide and seek under this exact rule set" (r/JLTG/1qg8gtv).
  - Ben on the street-trace question: "we don't really love it." In dense street grids it's slow to match and the shape isn't unique (r/JLTG/1hez23x).
- **Fixes fans proposed [F]:**
  - Let the hider ban questions at the start of a round, like a free Drained Brain (r/JLTG/1qgncjg).
  - Seekers draw 3 questions and pick 1.
  - Category cooldowns or forced rotation between categories.
  - In the endgame: live photos only and 1-minute answers, and no curses that pull seekers out of the zone (r/JLTG/1kpunif).
  - Fairer restart points between runs.

## 5. The home game

**How it was adapted [V]** ([rules](https://jetlag.denull.ru/en/rules/))

- **Three sizes:**
  - Small: a town; 4–8 hours; 30–100 stations; 30-minute hide.
  - Medium: a city such as NYC; about a day; 100–500 stations; 60-minute hide.
  - Large: a country; 2–4 days; 500+ stations; 3-hour hide.
- Card values and curse parameters scale with game size.
- **Fairness rules:**
  - Everyone uses the same maps app.
  - Places on Google Maps count only if they have 5+ reviews.
  - The seekers are responsible for making their questions unambiguous.
  - Exclude any area where a player wouldn't feel safe.
  - Pausing is allowed, but not for strategic advantage.
- **Optional variants** in the rulebook:
  - Playing by car.
  - Two seeker teams scored on fastest seeking time.
  - A "global" version, which comes with the advice "Don't do it."
- **Product:**
  - Base game: $35, 2–4 players, 125 cards, an Investigation Book of questions, 6 dice ([store](https://store.nebula.tv/products/jet-lag-the-game-hide-and-seek-transit-game)).
  - Expansion: $15, 95 cards including 50 curses, sold as "a toolkit… to customize."

**Reception [V + F]**

- **BoardGameGeek [V]:** the game isn't listed. A submission was rejected under BGG's "played on a tabletop" rule, and the only thread is about whether it should be eligible ([BGG](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3517477)).
- **Player reports [F]:**
  - Little downtime, even while hiding (r/JLTG/1kkobp4).
  - In small games, 10-minute answers combined with vetoes drag. Overlapping zones blur the cuts, and photos taken in advance are a problem (r/JLTG/1kpunif).
  - "The endgame was the least interesting part" ([tildes](https://tildes.net/~hobbies/1nsf/we_played_jetlag_hide_seek_and_it_went_well)).

## What this suggests for your NYC game [I]

- **Scoring:** having questions add flat minutes to the hider's score is basically the time-bonus-only version. Ben's criticism of the dice applies: give the hider choices. For example, keep 1 of N cards, offer veto and randomize, and gate curses behind casting costs.
- **Cooldown:** your global 5-minute cooldown is much gentler than Switzerland's 30-minute per-category cooldown. A per-category cooldown would break up the radar and thermometer routine.
- **Expensive questions:** the platform-tile and "does route X stop there" questions nearly decided the NYC round. Price them high. Note that the official transit-line question already says an express that skips the station is "no."
- **Zones:** centre them on a station, the same change Ben called a cleaner rule.
- **Street View:** the show bans it for seekers and only uses it inside a curse, so a Street View endgame is new. To keep it from dragging, give it answer timers or limit how many questions can be asked there.
