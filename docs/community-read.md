## What it means

1. No "I made a game" post. Every one this year followed the same arc, and the mods are limiting them.
2. One data post into the rules conversation, before Season 20 airs: the 780-round sim, what a min-maxing seeker asks, what a veto is worth, which stations can't be found. The game is a footnote. Ask the mods first.
3. Answer the AI question honestly if asked, in a comment: the design, rules, balance and look were decided by Charlie; AI did the implementation; the docs are public.
4. Show what slop can't: no show cards or logos (say so), the Mosaic look (lead with a screenshot), longevity (a changelog page, the daily number).
5. Lead with "You hide": the sub's own diagnosis is that the hider has no agency.
